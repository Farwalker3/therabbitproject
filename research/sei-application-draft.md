## Track B — Direct Foundation application (structured ask)

### Project

**The Rabbit Project — Revival.** Reviving an abandoned Sei-native NFT
game as a living collection, serialized story, and community on Sei — and
as an animated series that carries Sei's name beyond crypto.

### The story (why this is a Sei application, not a generic one)

The Rabbit Project lived at rabbit.game and on Sei (@RabbitsOnSei), with a
token CoinGecko indexed as `the-rabbit-project`. It was an idle game with
a built-in chat and trade system: unique NFT rabbits explored dungeons for
$CARROT, healed at the Hospital, crafted at the Blacksmith with gems from
World Map raids, bred rabbits with inheritable traits, raced them, and
built clans. It was abandoned: domains dead, docs surviving only as
search-index fragments, its CryptoGames listing frozen at "0 reviews."

We recovered everything that survives — complete archives of the last two
public listings, the original artwork (banner, the carrot-and-sword
emblem, marks), and the reconstructed game canon — all public at
github.com/Farwalker3/therabbitproject, alongside a revival site and a
complete six-episode animated series treatment whose premise is the
project's real fate: **a world that kept running after everyone logged
off.**

A dead project on any chain is a quiet embarrassment. A community-led
revival is the opposite: proof that things built on Sei don't stay dead —
someone catches them. That's the story this grant funds, and it's a story
only Sei can host.

### What this grant funds (on Sei, 6 months, $25,000)

**Milestone 1 — Foundation (months 1–2, $8,000)**
- Community resurrection: Discord, X, revival site live; outreach to
  original players/holders and The Rabbit's Club community (2,250-holder
  hand-drawn rabbit collection we're in early conversations with).
- On-chain archaeology report: identify the original collection/token
  contracts via Sei explorers (SeiScan / Seitrace, Cosmos- and EVM-side),
  snapshot original holders if recoverable — published openly.
- Story bible v1 + serialized story drop #1 from the recovered canon.

**Milestone 2 — The collection comes home (months 3–4, $9,000)**
- Revival collection as ERC-721 on Sei EVM: testnet, then mainnet.
- Original-holder recognition: if the archaeology identifies holders, they
  claim free; the revival honors the dead world's citizens first.
- Story drops #2–4; collection art extending the recovered originals.

**Milestone 3 — The flywheel (months 5–6, $8,000)**
- Animated teaser storyboard/animatic from the series treatment — every
  frame of the show's development markets a collection that lives on Sei.
- The Revival Project registry (farwalker3.github.io/revival-project)
  commits excavation #002 to another dead Sei project — the methodology,
  productized, starts paying Sei back systematically.
- Public metrics report: community size, holders, story readership.

**Budget:** collection contracts + review $8k · story/content production
$7k · community operations (6 months) $6k · open-source archival/registry
tooling $4k.

### What this grant does NOT fund (disclosure)

- The playable game client rebuild — pursued separately via gaming-specific
  grants (a SKALE application is submitted).
- Substrate engine research (parked pending Polkadot program availability).
- The animated series production itself — the series is developed with
  media partners; this grant only touches the storyboard/animatic tied to
  the Sei collection.

No deliverable overlaps with any other application; we're happy to walk
through the funding map.

### The honest IP position

Our model is acquisition-first — think of it as a buyback for worlds: dead
projects usually still have owners, and The Revival Project's fund exists
to find them and buy the IP properly. For The Rabbit Project specifically,
Milestone 1's on-chain archaeology doubles as the owner search: domains
are dead and no surviving legal entity has been found so far, so until an
owner surfaces we claim revival stewardship, not original ownership — the
recovered materials are archived publicly and credited to the original
creators, and if any original team member resurfaces, the door is open
(and the fund is ready). The series and all new art are original work.
