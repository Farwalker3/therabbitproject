# The Rabbit Project — Recovery Dossier

Compiled 2026-07-11; updated same day after complete saved copies of both
surviving listings were added to this repo (see `archive/`). This document
collects everything recoverable about **The Rabbit Project** (rabbit.game),
an abandoned blockchain idle game, as source material for its revival as a
story / TV show.

---

## 1. What the project was

**The Rabbit Project** was a play-to-earn **idle game** with a built-in **chat
and trade system**. Players controlled **unique NFT rabbit characters**,
explored **dungeons**, collected the in-game currency **$CARROT**, and engaged
in activities to enhance their characters and their **clans**.

> "The Rabbit Project is an idle game with built-in chat and trade system,
> where players control unique NFT characters to explore dungeons, collect
> $CARROT and engage in various activities to enhance their characters and
> clans."
> — official description (indexed from gitbook.rabbit.game and mirrored on
> ChainPlay / CryptoGames)

**Primary goal**: build a powerful clan by leveling up characters,
participating in clan activities, and optimizing dungeon clears to maximize
$CARROT gains.

The CryptoGames review (preserved in `archive/`) adds a second layer of
mechanics:

> ""The Rabbit Project" is a blockchain game that allows players to collect,
> breed, and trade virtual rabbits. Players can purchase unique rabbits with
> varying traits, such as colors and patterns, and breed them to create new
> and rare rabbits… The game also features a competitive element, with
> players able to participate in rabbit races and other challenges to earn
> rewards."

So the full system set is: **idle dungeon-grinding + clans + breeding (trait
inheritance: colors, patterns) + rabbit races + trading**.

## 2. The game world — in-game locations (recovered from the official GitBook)

| Location | Function |
|---|---|
| **Hospital** | Fix up your injured rabbits by paying with $CARROT |
| **Arena** | Let your rabbits battle it out with other clans |
| **Main Building** | Arrange and set up your clans here |
| **Blacksmith** | Craft and upgrade items using $CARROT and types of gems |
| **World Map** | Send rabbits on raids to earn $CARROT and gems |

## 3. Economy & systems

- **$CARROT** — the core currency. Earned from dungeon clears and raids;
  spent at the Hospital (healing), the Blacksmith (crafting/upgrading), and in
  clan activities.
- **Gems** — secondary resources, in multiple types, earned from World Map
  raids and consumed by the Blacksmith for crafting and upgrades.
- **Injury system** — rabbits come back from raids/dungeons injured and must
  be healed at the Hospital before working again.
- **Breeding** — rabbits carry traits (colors, patterns); breeding produces
  new and rarer rabbits.
- **Races** — competitive rabbit races and challenges award rewards.
- **Clans** — the central social unit: arranged in the Main Building, pitted
  against each other in the Arena, progressed through clan activities.
- **Trade & chat** — built into the game client; NFT rabbits were tradeable.

## 7. Notes for the revival

The historical arc of the project itself is the story hook: a world of
working rabbits built to grind forever, whose players — the reason for all
the grinding — quietly vanished. The game kept idling; nobody was watching.
That premise (abandonment of a persistent world, seen from inside) drives the
TV-show treatment on the revival site (`index.html`).

## 8. Potential partner: The Rabbit's Club

A separate, **living** rabbit NFT community identified as a pitch target and
co-development partner for the show.

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Collection | 2,250 hand-drawn NFTs ("inspired by prairie critters"), each rabbit and its elements drawn by hand |
| Blockchain | Ethereum |
| Launched | May 31, 2024 |
| Theme | Folklore: rabbits' origins, lifestyles, and "connections to mysteries, cultures, and legends worldwide" |
| Community model | Holders interact with **the Storyteller**, who develops the narrative with the community (discussions, theories, public canon) |
| Recognition | Featured on NFT Design Awards: https://nftdesignawards.io/project/the-rabbits-club |
| X / Twitter | https://x.com/TheRabbit_Club |
| Sample art | `assets/rabbits-club-art.webp` |

**Why they fit**: they have living hand-drawn art, an award-listed identity,
and — most importantly — a community already practiced at co-writing lore
through the Storyteller. The Rabbit Project has the world, mechanics, and
premise but no living community. The crossover conceit: the legend of the
abandoned warren becomes a tale the Storyteller tells — their folklore frames
the show.

**Open questions for outreach**: team identities, OpenSea collection link,
holder count / floor activity, whether the Storyteller is one person or a
rotating role, rights posture on the art.
